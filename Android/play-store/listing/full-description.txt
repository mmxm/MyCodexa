Codexa is a self-hosted EPUB and comic book reader you connect to your own Codexa server — this app is a client, not a standalone library. You (or someone you trust) run the free, open-source Codexa server on a home NAS, small VPS, or similar, then read from it here, offline-capable, with your highlights and progress synced across every device.

IMPORTANT: This app requires a Codexa server to connect to. It is not a book store and does not include any books. Learn how to set up a server at the project's GitHub/Codeberg page (linked below).

WHY CODEXA
• Built on CXReader, Codexa's own EPUB engine — replaces generic third-party libraries with something built for reliability, faithful formatting, and real-world messy EPUBs, fixed-layout comics, and CBZ/CBR files alike.
• Books and comics live in one library — first-class EPUB, fixed-layout EPUB, CBZ, and CBR support, no separate apps needed.
• True two-way KOReader sync — a built-in KOSync-compatible server keeps your e-reader and phone on the same page, no extra software, no cloud.
• Multi-user with per-user libraries, progress, and settings.
• Your data stays yours — no cloud account, no tracking, no ads. Everything (books, covers, highlights, progress) lives on your own server.

READING
• Exact position restore — reopens on the precise page, not just an approximate percentage
• Bookmarks, and highlights in four colours with optional notes
• Full-text in-book search
• Built-in dictionary lookup with local StarDict dictionaries, including proper Chinese/Japanese/Korean word segmentation
• Footnote and endnote popups without leaving the page
• Bionic reading, two-page spread, fullscreen mode, auto-hiding toolbar
• Page-turn animations: none, fade, slide, paper, momentum, or zoom

OFFLINE & MOBILE
• Full offline reading once a book is downloaded
• Volume-key page turning, sleep timer, sync status indicators
• Home-screen widget-style resume banner if you close mid-chapter

THEMES & DISPLAY
• Seven reading themes (Light, Sepia, Dark, Sepia Dark, Midnight, Nord) plus a fully custom theme
• Dedicated e-ink mode: high-contrast black-and-white for e-ink displays
• One-tap UI scaling that works from phones to tablets

LIBRARY & CATALOGUES
• Browse and download from any OPDS catalogue (Calibre-Web, Komga, Kavita, Ubooquity, and more)
• Optional BookOrbit integration: browse a self-hosted BookOrbit server's libraries, preview books before downloading, and sync highlights, reading sessions, and progress two-way
• Shelves, series support, sort & search, reading statistics

ACCOUNTS
• Multi-user with JWT authentication
• Optional single sign-on (Google, Apple, or a self-hosted provider) alongside local accounts, if your admin enables it
